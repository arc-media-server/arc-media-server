# Bug hunt: matching, the poster menu and flaky browser tests

The owner asked for a deep look for bugs. What was checked: `go vet`, every Go test under the
race detector (30 packages, no races), the full browser suite, a scripted walk through every
page at desktop and phone width (no browser errors, failed requests or sideways overflow), and a
throwaway Arc with the test library plus real TMDB and TheTVDB lookups: scanning, the TV
orderings, a drive going offline and coming back (history kept), a film dropped into a folder
(found in 10 seconds), odd API input (no server errors), and matching for 45 well-known films
and 25 shows that commonly trip media servers up.

## Matching (the most important finds)

Real lookups left Alien (in the test library) and 7 of 45 well-known films unidentified:
Arrival, Big, Crash, Dune (2021), Halloween (2018), It (2017) and Rocky. TMDB returned the right
film first each time, but `confidentMatch` only compared titles: a same-named film from another year (Dune 1984, the 1978
Halloween), an obscure same-named film from the same year (a second "Rocky" from 1976), or one
with an article ("The Alien", 1979) made it give up. Now, when titles tie, more evidence decides,
never result order alone:

1. the file's year: results more than a year away don't count;
2. one of the closest titles far better known than every other (at least 100 TMDB votes and ten
   times any other);
3. the exact year;
4. the title word for word, articles included.

Anything still tied stays for Fix Match, as before (two well-known films of one name and year;
"The Thing" without a year).

TV: TheTVDB tells same-named shows apart in the name ("Doctor Who (2005)", "The Office (US)"),
so its own search found none of the newer halves of five such pairs; they were only identified
through TMDB's cross-references. These tags are now ignored when comparing (the year, or the
same tag in the folder name, still decides). A folder with a subtitle the providers leave out
("Cosmos - A Spacetime Odyssey", known as "Cosmos" from 2014) matches the main title, but only
the single candidate from exactly that year.

Result: 45 of 45 films and 25 of 25 shows identified, every one checked against the right TMDB or
TheTVDB id, and the 38 films that matched before match the same titles. `TestMatchingConfidenceCorpus`
gained 17 cases from these runs.

## Poster ⋯ menu (from #266)

- Add to / Remove from Watchlist didn't refresh anything: reopening the menu still said "Add to
  Watchlist", and lists kept the old state. It now refreshes the watchlist and the title lists.
- A note from an action finishing after the menu had closed (a show's Play next looks its
  episodes up first) could be lost, and parts of the menu stayed built after a queue or analyze
  action. Notes now live with the card, and the menu stays built only while an action is pending
  or a dialog is open.
- Library page: the loaded titles were rebuilt as a new list on every render, which also re-set
  the A–Z rail's scroll tracking each time. The list is now only rebuilt when a page arrives.

A browser journey covers the Watchlist and late-note cases; it fails against the old code.

## Flaky browser journeys

Three journeys failed in nearly every full run but passed alone (series More menu, queue editing,
compact playback's next-episode countdown; also the preplay episode header once). The two
"merging shows" journeys add a second "Arc Show" folder to the media shared by every test and
never removed it, so later tests found two shows of that name and, with equal titles and years,
picked either at random. The maintenance tests' fixture now removes that folder after each test.
Not a product bug.

## Checked and fine

Drive offline and back (watch progress and rating kept), automatic pick-up of new files,
Intended Order filling once chosen (deep mapping, by design), idle conversions ended after two
minutes, database result sets all closed, no one-line `useEffect` returning a value, Specials
listed last (deliberate). Heat's orange poster is the test library's own `poster.jpg`.
