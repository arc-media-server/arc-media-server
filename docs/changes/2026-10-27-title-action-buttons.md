# Title action buttons (27 October 2026, PR #224)

The owner asked for consistency. On movie and show pages Play (or Play S1 E1 / Resume) is the
only text button; Playback options, Shuffle, Trailer, Watched, Watchlist, Playback queue, Add to
Playlist and More are matching 48px square icon buttons with hover tooltips and unchanged
accessible names. Episode rows keep their compact icons.
